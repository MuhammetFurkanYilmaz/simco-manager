## 🎉 v1.1.22 - Core Stability & Bug Fixes

### 🚀 Improvements
* **Performance Optimization:** We've drastically reduced the frequency of background data synchronization (now every 24 hours), significantly reducing CPU and memory usage to ensure your browser runs smoother.
* **Bug Fixes:**
  * Resolved various UI rendering issues for a cleaner visual experience.
  * Fixed an issue causing calculation problems in the Financials Helper.
* **New Features:**
  * Added a direct Feedback Button! You can now easily send us your thoughts, report issues, or suggest new features right from the extension.
  * You can now upload `.csv` files directly to the Financials Helper for offline data analysis and reporting.
